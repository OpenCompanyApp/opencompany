# Commercial Use

Status: Prospective commercial-use guidance for a future release distributed with the current root
`LICENSE.md`; historical releases retain their shipped terms.
Last verified: 2026-11-02.

OpenCompany's planned `AGPL-3.0-only` release is open source. Commercial use is allowed; a separate
commercial license is not required merely because an organization uses, modifies, redistributes,
embeds, white-labels, or hosts the covered software. The [license text](LICENSE.md) controls.

## Common allowed commercial uses

Subject to the AGPL terms, you may:

- self-host OpenCompany for one organization or a group of organizations;
- sell installation, migration, implementation, training, support, and managed-operation services;
- modify OpenCompany for internal or customer use;
- operate a paid hosted OpenCompany service or a competing service;
- distribute a branded fork under a non-confusing name;
- build independent products and integrations against public protocols and SDKs; and
- charge for infrastructure, compute, storage, models, support, certification, or service levels.

## Obligations that commercial use does not remove

AGPL obligations still apply. In particular, an operator of a modified network version must offer
Corresponding Source to users interacting with it as required by section 13. Conveyed copies and
covered combinations need the applicable source, notices, and license. Do not use a private plugin
or service boundary as a label for code that actually modifies, links with, or copies covered
implementation.

The exact scope is architecture- and fact-dependent; obtain legal advice for a specific product.

## Official commercial products

Ginger Media BV or a future publishing entity may sell:

- OpenCompany Cloud and managed infrastructure, including managed per-agent computers (planned;
  self-hosters can attach their own computer providers);
- deployment, migration, operations, security response, and support;
- service levels, compliance assistance, training, and certification;
- managed AI inference, storage, automation, and Bowerbird execution; and
- marketplace, partner, and professional-service offerings.

Customers pay for an excellent official service and accountable operation, not for permission to
exercise rights already granted by AGPL.

Separate Cloud billing, fleet orchestration, abuse prevention, private deployment automation, and
company-internal systems may remain proprietary when they are independent works across documented
interfaces. They may not silently privatize modifications to the AGPL-covered application.

## Historical versions

Versions already published under BSL 1.1 retain the BSL terms and future-license dates shipped with
them. Any version actually published under FSL retains its FSL and Apache future-license terms. The
new AGPL policy applies prospectively only when a release ships with that license.

## Trademarks

The AGPL grants copyright and patent rights, not a general right to present a fork or service as
official OpenCompany. Truthful compatibility and origin statements are allowed under the separate
[Trademark and Compatibility Policy](TRADEMARKS.md).
