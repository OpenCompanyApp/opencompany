# OpenCompany Trademark and Compatibility Policy

Status: Interim policy prepared for the prospective AGPL-3.0-only release; legal review is required before
publication.
Last verified: 2026-09-01.

The OpenCompany software license grants copyright and patent permissions subject to its terms. It
does not grant a general license to the OpenCompany names, logos, product identity, domains, or
other source-identifying marks (the "OpenCompany Marks"). Ginger Media BV reserves those rights.

## Uses that do not require separate permission

Subject to applicable trademark law, you may:

- use the word OpenCompany truthfully to identify the unmodified software and its origin;
- link to the official project or describe factual compatibility with OpenCompany;
- state that a project is "based on OpenCompany" or "compatible with OpenCompany" when that claim
  is accurate and not misleading;
- reproduce a textual OpenCompany reference required by the software license or an attribution
  notice; and
- discuss, review, teach, or report on OpenCompany.

Use only as much of the mark as is reasonably necessary. Do not style a compatibility statement as
an official product name, use an OpenCompany logo as the prominent identity of a fork, or imply
sponsorship, certification, or endorsement.

## Uses that require written permission

Unless applicable law independently permits the use, obtain written permission before you:

- name a fork, hosted service, company, product, or package OpenCompany or a confusingly similar
  name;
- use an OpenCompany logo or visual identity as your product or service branding;
- register a domain name, organization, social account, application listing, or marketplace entry
  that is likely to appear official;
- describe a modified distribution or third-party service as the official OpenCompany release or
  OpenCompany Cloud; or
- use an OpenCompany Mark in merchandise, advertising, or a way that suggests a commercial
  relationship with Ginger Media BV.

## Forks and compatible products

A fork or independently operated service should use its own primary name and visual identity. It
may use a factual statement such as "based on OpenCompany" in secondary text when it also makes
clear who operates and supports that product. Remove OpenCompany logos and official-service
branding from a modified product unless written permission says otherwise.

Compatibility claims must identify the relevant interface or version when that matters and must
not imply that Ginger Media BV tested or certified the product.

## No effect on statutory rights

This policy does not limit uses that applicable law permits without a trademark license. It does
not alter the copyright license in [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md), grant rights in third-party marks, or
constitute legal advice.
