1. Purpose

This policy sets list prices, discount limits and payment terms for all Acme agency work, so that quotes are consistent whether a person or an agent drafts them.

2. Price list

PackageScopePrice
StarterDiscovery + one-page site€4,500 €4,900

Prices exclude VAT. Reverse-charge clients: see the quote calculator.

3. Discounts

Account owners may give up to 15%10% without approval. Discounts above 20% need the sales lead. This is enforced as a business rule on the Deals discount field, for people and agents alike. ✓✕A

Multi-year contracts get an extra 5% per year committed, capped at 10%.

4. Payment terms

Invoices are due in 3014 days. Reminders go out at 7 and 14 days overdue as drafts for the account owner to send. ✓✕A
