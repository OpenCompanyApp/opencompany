How should Acme be governed?
Presets set defaults only. You can switch any time, every setting stays tunable, and all three are free and included in the self-hosted product.
◔
Personal
Just you and your agents
  • You are the only approver
  • Approval cards in the agent's DM, on Home and Telegram
  • Per-agent “always allow” list and autonomy slider
  • No role or policy screens
  • Deletes go to a 30-day trash automatically
Like a personal agent app, with the same safety floor.
◑
Team Recommended
About 2–50 people
  • Owner, Admin and Member roles; Spaces carry access
  • Each agent's manager is its default approver
  • Personal or shared connections; a budget per agent
  • Bulk edits over ~50 rows and schema changes become proposals
  • Adds Admin → Governance: policies, exceptions, approvers, budgets, audit
Recommended because you invited 2 people.
●
Company
Larger or regulated teams
  • Approver policies per Space and action class
  • Two approvers for purge, spend above a threshold and exposure
  • Admin-approved connection and MCP allowlists
  • Computer-attach policy and skill approval
  • Audit export and retention settings
Routine-started external sends only reach allowlisted targets.
