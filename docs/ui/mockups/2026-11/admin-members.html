Roles in the Team preset
Owner · everything Admin can do, plus full exports with secrets, workspace deletion and transferring ownership.
Admin · connections, hosts, governance, budgets, members, exposure approvals. Cannot approve their own requests.
Member · works in the Spaces they belong to, manages the agents they are manager of, grants “always” on their own personal connections.
ⓘBusiness roles such as sales-lead only name approvers for field rules. They grant no extra access.
Agents profiles live in Team · managers are their default approvers
